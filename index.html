Project 3 · AEO

Answer-first FAQ with schema

Answer engines and featured snippets favor a direct answer near the top, followed by detail. Below is a sample block for the question "How do I brew coffee with a V60?"

How do I brew coffee with a V60?

Use 15 g of medium-fine coffee and 250 g of water at about 94 °C. Wet the filter, bloom the grounds with 40 g of water for 30 seconds, then pour the rest in slow circles. Total brew time is about three minutes.

Highlighted: the 45-word direct answer. Below it, the page would add steps, gear, and troubleshooting.

<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "FAQPage",
  "mainEntity": [{
    "@type": "Question",
    "name": "How do I brew coffee with a V60?",
    "acceptedAnswer": {
      "@type": "Answer",
      "text": "Use 15 g of medium-fine coffee and 250 g of water at about 94 °C. Wet the filter, bloom the grounds with 40 g of water for 30 seconds, then pour the rest in slow circles. Total brew time is about three minutes."
    }
  }]
}
</script>

Google now shows FAQ rich results for only a small set of sites, so I treat this markup as a way to make the answer structure clear to machines, not as a promise of a rich result.

Project 5 · GEO

AI search visibility checklist

Generative engines pull from pages that are clear, specific and consistent across the web. This is the checklist I would run for a brand.

  • Entity claritySame brand name, description and location on the site, social profiles and directories.
  • Citable factsOriginal details such as roast dates, origins and brew ratios stated plainly in short paragraphs.
  • Structured pagesClear headings, definitions, tables and FAQs that an AI can lift as an answer.
  • Brand mention monitoringTest a fixed set of questions in AI tools each month and log whether the brand is mentioned or cited.
  • Citation opportunitiesList sites that AI answers already cite for the topic and note which ones could feature the brand.
  • Backlink researchCompare competitor backlinks and pick relevant outreach targets such as coffee blogs and local lifestyle sites.
